Overview

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus District, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science in Mengabang Telipot, Kuala Terengganu, providing facilities for both students and lecturers from the Fisheries and Marine Science programmes to conduct their practical sessions and research.

Eventually, the Marine Science and Fisheries faculty of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia in Serdang was transferred to Kuala Terengganu, and the centre was transformed into a branch campus, being named Universiti Putra Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT) in May 1996. Later, it was granted autonomy as Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) before finally adopting its current name, Universiti Malaysia Terengganu, on 1 February 2007.

Today, UMT stands as Malaysia's premier university focusing on marine science and aquatic resources, while offering a diverse range of academic programmes in science, technology, management, and maritime studies.
